Record Overview

The dossier examines a paying Gemini user's reported disappearance of a visible usage meter, restrictions on model selection and an instance of unavailable Pro access during a conversation conducted on Flash.

The source record comprises seven unmodified screenshots and a separate, unaltered 482-line Gemini transcript. The report reproduces these materials, documents the responses generated by Gemini and identifies specific account-level questions for Google.

Seven screenshots · One transcript · SHA-256 source and release manifests · Full PHI-RAID-616 Rev01 license

Executive Summary

Gemini supplied detailed explanations concerning subscription entitlements, model routing, usage accounting and restrictions while the user described an interface that no longer displayed a remaining allowance. One screenshot contains a generated block labeled DIAGNOSTIC_READOUT. The report preserves these outputs as evidence of what the assistant communicated, rather than as authenticated Google operational records.

The central questions are measurable: which models each paid service provides, what quantity is counted, whether Flash use can affect Pro availability, how restrictions are communicated and what account telemetry was actually available to Gemini. The next evidence stage is a proposed series of eight separate continuous recordings across distinct five-hour refresh windows; those recordings are not part of this release.
