# Supplemental report narrative for the FTC

**Subject:** Supplemental documentation of paid generative-AI subscription access, visibility of usage limits, and model availability — TM-GEMINI-2026-11-12

This submission supplements an earlier consumer-practices report concerning Google’s paid Gemini offering. Phi Labs Research has compiled a dated evidence dossier from the affected subscriber’s original materials: seven unmodified screenshots, a 482-line unaltered Gemini conversation transcript, a source-integrity manifest and a clinical exhibit-by-exhibit analysis.

The subscriber reports that a usage meter previously visible in the Gemini interface became unavailable, while restrictions on paid-model access remained enforceable. The subscriber also reports that Pro became unavailable after a conversation on Flash without Pro being used in that conversation. Separately, the subscriber reports personal AI Pro and Workspace Business Starter subscriptions with confusing model eligibility. Gemini itself generated highly specific explanations of metering, fallback and subscription tiers, including a text block styled as `DIAGNOSTIC_READOUT`; the dossier preserves these as the model’s claims, not authenticated disclosures from Google.

The issue for examination is whether the subscription’s practical model availability, usage quantity, reset timing and restrictions were clearly conveyed to paying consumers, especially where the interface no longer displayed a remaining allowance or where relative access multipliers lacked an intelligible baseline. This record does not ask the Commission to accept the assistant’s explanations as proof of Google’s internal practices; it supplies the original outputs and a set of specific questions resolvable using Google’s records.

The enclosed dossier identifies the requested disclosures and a prospective eight-session continuous recording protocol. The latter is a planned next evidence collection, not evidence claimed already to exist.
