# Google press inquiry / request for on-record response

**To:** press@google.com  
**Subject:** On-record questions concerning paid Gemini model access and hidden usage telemetry — Phi Labs Research dossier

Google Press Team,

Phi Labs Research, publisher of The Measure, is preparing a public evidence release concerning a paid Gemini user’s loss of visible usage accounting and disputed model availability. *Gemini Roasts Google: The Invisible Meter* (Rev02) contains seven unmodified screenshots, a complete 482-line Gemini conversation transcript, independent SHA-256 manifests, and evidence-specific analyses.

The record includes the user’s report of unavailable Pro access following Flash use, the reported disappearance of the usage meter, questions about account access across personal AI Pro and Workspace subscriptions, and a Gemini response styled as an account `DIAGNOSTIC_READOUT` whose source was not identified. The report requests answers that Google’s product records should be able to establish, without treating the chatbot’s generated explanations as verified operational disclosures.

We request an attributable product statement addressing the official quota-accounting unit and reset periods, whether Flash usage can affect Pro eligibility, where paid subscribers can inspect remaining allowance, how model-selection restrictions are communicated, and whether account-specific telemetry was available to Gemini in the displayed exchange.

**Record:** TM-GEMINI-2026-11-12  
**Evidence package:** available upon request or from the DOI record
