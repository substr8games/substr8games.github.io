A Public Interest Research Company

The Mandate

Phi Labs Research is a public interest research company established in 2026 and based in Los Angeles, California. Our mandate is to eradicate the beaurocratic walls that separate public inquiry and open discourse from institutions that possess and exercise power and authority over public-facing policy and influence the direction of society and government. Our goal and our mission statement is simple: we want to hold institutions accountable to the public commensurate with the power that they exercise. Our official tagline Making Accountability An Inevitability is as much a promise and an aspiration as it is a challenge to institutional authority that sidesteps, avoids, undermines, or otherwise refuses transparent operations with regard to policy and decision-making operations that affect the public.

The Covenant

Phi Labs exists to make accountability inevitable. We develop transparent, inspectable, and independently verifiable technologies that challenge the concentration of unaccountable power. We believe that authority must answer to evidence, that reasoning must withstand scrutiny, and that no institution should be exempt from the standards it imposes upon others. Our mission is to transform accountability from something the powerful may choose to permit into something they cannot escape.

Founding Axioms // Values

  • Because I can is not an answer.
  • Accountability is a prerequisite for authority, not a privilege to be granted.
  • Reason does not recognize rank. Power must show its work.
  • Rules must apply to their authors. Authority does not provide for exemption from rules established by that authority.
  • Knowledge must be able to defend itself. A claim should survive separation from its original authority.
  • Independence is a condition of freedom. A person can not meaningfully engage with opaque, incomprehensible systems that deny them access.
  • Transparency must serve the individual, not expose them.
  • Build for inevitability. Temporary solutions are not real solutions. An exception carved out for authority directly undermines a real solution.

The Standard Is the Work.

Not the institution endorsing it.

Phi Labs does not seek academic approval, institutional recognition, or permission to investigate, invent, question, or publish. No institution possesses exclusive authority to determine the legitimacy of an idea, the validity of an investigation, or the usefulness of a result. Those judgments belong to the evidence, the methodology, and what the work demonstrably establishes or enables.

We reject the proposition that intellectual legitimacy must be conferred by the very institutions whose structures, practices, and authority we may be examining.

We apply the same standard to ourselves. Our research, engineering, publications, and institutional practices are subject to examination, criticism, and correction. We claim no exemption from the accountability we demand of others.

We do not ask for recognition. We produce work that can be examined.

What We Do

Phi Labs Research investigates how institutions exercise power and builds tools that make their decisions easier to examine. We trace who can make a decision, what rules govern it, how it is enforced, and who bears the consequences. We publish our findings so the people affected can inspect the evidence, challenge our reasoning, and use what we find.

The Measure is our public research and editorial publication. The question we keep returning to is: what gives an institution the right to do what it has the power to do? This question sits at the intersection of philosophy, ethics, and law. The structural analysis traces how a decision gets made and enforced. The legal inquiry asks where the authority comes from and what limits it. The ethical inquiry asks whether its exercise can be justified to the people who bear the consequences.

An Open Invitation

Phi Labs Research is a public interest research company. We welcome corrections, counterexamples, and substantive rebuttals when the public is included in the discussion and allowed to participate. Authors are encouraged to publish their responses openly and share the link publicly. Private correspondence will likely go ignored.
